# Arenyxa name, logo, and distribution identity

Arenyxa source code is licensed under **GPL-3.0-or-later**. That license intentionally permits studying, modifying, redistributing, and charging for copies or services, provided the GPL obligations are satisfied.

The software's **verified-official** badge is different from the GPL license. It is a cryptographic provenance statement: it means the installed files correspond to a release manifest signed by a key explicitly trusted by that build of Arenyxa.

To preserve user freedom while reducing misleading commercial redistribution:

- Modified builds remain fully functional and may be redistributed under the GPL, but should identify themselves as modified/community builds rather than claiming verified-official provenance.
- Third-party distributors must preserve the GPL notices and corresponding-source obligations that apply to their distribution.
- The Arenyxa name/logo should not be used in a way that falsely implies endorsement, official support, or an unmodified official release.
- Forks are encouraged to keep compatibility and attribution while using a distinct distribution identity when they materially modify the product.
- No hardware binding, online activation, forced account, telemetry gate, or feature lock is used to enforce provenance.

Cryptographic release signing protects provenance; it does **not** convert GPL software into DRM and does not prohibit lawful commercial distribution.
